# K53 Study Guide — storefront review

Prepared 8 October 2026. **Approved by the user on 8 October 2026. Nothing uploaded, saved to either store, or published.**

The package contains **42 screenshots across six size families, a Google Play feature graphic, an Apple header and an optional Apple search-results graphic**. All landscape graphics retain the smiling lady, following your feedback. English (UK) copy and captions are the first release package; in-app language choice is shown explicitly.

## Positioning and changes

**Prepare for your learner’s licence with confidence.** The first three images tell the core story: study, practise, then try a structured mock test. The following images show useful content, section results, four languages and light/dark mode. The blue/indigo/teal road-and-sunrise artwork connects the storefronts to the app’s palette.

The refreshed copy puts the learner’s task first, makes progress and randomized practice concrete, and clearly separates free study/quizzes from Premium mock tests. The title adds a readable search-intent phrase while retaining the existing brand. Apple’s keyword field avoids repeating words already present in its title and subtitle. These are relevance choices, not a promise of higher ranking. No measured conversion uplift is claimed.

Keep the current K53 icon: the repository’s 512-pixel Play icon and 1,024-pixel iOS build icon visually match. A new icon would need a coordinated native build update; the approved scope refreshes the surrounding creative.

After an approved launch, compare discovery, listing conversion and acquisitions over comparable 28-day windows, separately for each store. This release and creative refresh are a combined change; do not attribute results to one asset. See [the strategy and initial audit](PLAN.md).

```text
Prepare for your South African learner's licence with K53 Study Guide. Learn the road signs and rules, practise with randomized quizzes, and see your study progress as you go.

STUDY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Work through road signs, road markings, road signals, rules of the road, vehicle controls and defensive driving. Track the material you have covered and return to your last study topic when you are ready to continue.

MAKE PRACTICE PART OF YOUR ROUTINE
Choose the topics you want to practise and answer randomized quiz questions. Use short, focused sessions to build familiarity with the material.

GET FAMILIAR WITH A MOCK-TEST FORMAT
With Premium, practise a structured 64-question mock test across three sections: vehicle controls, rules of the road and road signs. Review your answers and section results to decide what to study next.

CHOOSE YOUR LANGUAGE AND YOUR LOOK
Use the app in English, Afrikaans, isiZulu or isiXhosa. Switch between light and dark mode to suit your study time.

WHAT IS FREE?
Study material, progress tracking and quizzes are free. Premium is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks mock tests and lets you reset your study, quiz and test history. No subscription is required.

Start with one topic. Keep practising. Build confidence for test day.
```

- **PASS:** 45 exact-size RGB JPEGs, no alpha, all below 8 MB (the Play feature is also below its 15 MB limit). Final assets total approximately 10.6 MB. See [machine-readable asset checks](qa/asset-validation.json).
- **PASS:** all ten metadata/release-note fields fit the applicable limits. See [copy counts](qa/copy-validation.json).
- **PASS:** all final app panels preserve the source aspect ratio, fit fully inside their canvases and remain separate from marketing text. Fonts and images loaded before export. Tablet images come from actual tablet-width browser layouts.
- **Reviewed critically:** Image Gen’s pilot modified progress bars; its UI rendering was rejected. Only the surrounding artwork was retained. Screenshot captions were then typeset consistently over generated backgrounds, and the untouched source captures were placed inside. Landscape wording, character appearance, margins and spelling were visually reviewed. Opaque-windscreen alternatives were rejected after your feedback. See [production critique](qa/CREATIVE-REVIEW.md).
- **Build source verified:** Azure run `20261008.1` / build `1805` reported **Success** and source `390d468cc519e6f6c965bd0bbc3b784daa4a21ef`, matching these captures. Capture build was **1.40 (40)**. The subsequent result-navigation fix advances the next build to **1.40 (41)** without changing the screenshot layouts. That new Azure build is still to be run; the earlier success is not validation of build 41.
- **Before publication:** App Store Connect still has a **1.3** draft with no selected build. Reconcile that version with **1.40**, select the intended **build 41** after the next Azure run, and compare these web captures with the installed iOS/Android release apps. Do not submit the old draft unchanged.
- **Language limitation observed:** a supplementary isiXhosa dashboard capture still shows an English progress-summary phrase. The storefront image uses the genuine four-language selector; the copy promises language choice, not that every phrase has been independently translation-reviewed. Supplemental translated captures remain in `source-captures/*/localized.jpg` for inspection.

The app’s compact phone layout naturally scrolls some topics on short screens; the tablet layout has more white space. Those are genuine app behaviours and were not painted out. The user interface remains exact; only its uniform size, rounded outer corners and shadow change in the promotional canvas.
