Before you get started.

What can I use for free?

All six study topics, randomised practice quizzes and progress tracking. Premium adds full mock tests and the option to reset your study, quiz and test history.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time in-app purchase. Check the price in the app before buying. If you’ve already purchased, use Restore Purchases with the same store account.

Which languages are available?

English, Afrikaans, isiZulu and isiXhosa. Choose your preferred language in Profile.

Is this an official test or a pass guarantee?

K53 Study Guide is an independent study aid, not a government service or an official test provider. Mock tests help you practise and spot gaps; results do not guarantee a pass in your learner’s licence test.
