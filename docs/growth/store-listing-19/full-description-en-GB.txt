Prepare for your South African learner's licence test with K53 Study Guide. Study road signs, rules of the road and vehicle controls, then check your understanding with practice quizzes.

Study content and practice quizzes are free to use.

Premium access is a one-time purchase that unlocks full mock tests and lets you reset your study and assessment history. There is no subscription. Mock tests show a result for each section so you can see where to focus next.

Top Features:

#1. Study the K53 topics
Work through road signs, road markings, road signals, rules of the road, vehicle controls and defensive driving topics at your own pace.

#2. Practice with quizzes
Build confidence with configurable quizzes across the topics you need to improve. Track your progress as you study and practise.

#3. Mock learner's licence tests (Premium access)
Write full mock tests across the same types of sections used in the learner's licence test, including rules of the road, road signs and vehicle controls.

K53 Study Guide includes:
- K53 study material for South Africa
- Road signs, road markings and road signals
- Rules of the road and vehicle controls
- Learner's licence practice questions
- Configurable quizzes
- Mock practice tests with Premium access
- Progress tracking for study sections, quizzes and tests

The app is built for South African learners who want a focused way to prepare before booking or writing their learner's licence test.
