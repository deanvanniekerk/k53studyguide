# K53 Study Guide

K53 Study Guide helps learners prepare for the South African learner’s licence test. The mobile app and its website share this vocabulary.

## Language

**Learner**:
A person studying for the learner’s licence test using the app.
_Avoid_: Customer when describing study activity rather than a purchase.

**Study content**:
Learning material covering road signs, rules of the road and vehicle controls.
_Avoid_: Completed lesson merely because content became visible.

**Practice quiz**:
A set of practice questions used to check understanding while studying.
_Avoid_: Mock test, official test.

**Mock test**:
A practice assessment organised into the learner’s-test sections, with a result for each section.
_Avoid_: Practice quiz, official test.

**Premium access**:
The one-time paid unlock that provides mock tests and the ability to reset study and assessment history.
_Avoid_: Subscription, recurring plan.

**Premium offer**:
The presentation of premium benefits and the current purchase price to a learner.
_Avoid_: Purchase when only the offer has been viewed.

**Purchase attempt**:
A learner’s attempt to buy premium access through the platform store. It can succeed, be cancelled, remain pending or fail.
_Avoid_: Completed purchase before the store confirms success.

**Premium entitlement**:
The right to use the premium features following a recognised purchase.
_Avoid_: Payment when describing access rights.

**Restore purchase**:
Recovery of premium access already bought through the current platform store account.
_Avoid_: Repurchase, new sale.

**Legacy premium access**:
Premium access bought before the current purchase integration, which existing buyers must retain during migration.
_Avoid_: Free access, trial.

**Purchase cancellation**:
An attempt the learner dismisses or cancels before completion.
_Avoid_: Refund, purchase failure.

**Pending purchase**:
A store purchase awaiting a final outcome, during which new premium access has not yet been confirmed.
_Avoid_: Successful purchase, failed purchase.

**Store referral click**:
A click from the website to the app’s store listing.
_Avoid_: Install, acquisition.
